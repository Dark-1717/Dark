Our Premier Package: 7-Day Sri Lanka Highlights

Day 1: Arrival in Sri Lanka

Arrival: Arrive at Bandaranaike International Airport (CMB) in the evening.

Transfer to Negombo: After immigration and baggage collection, transfer to Negombo, a coastal town just 20–30 minutes from the airport.

Check-in: Arrive at your hotel in Negombo and check-in.

Evening at Leisure: Relax at the hotel after your journey. Optional short walk on the beach or a nearby local restaurant if time permits.

Overnight: Hotel in Negombo

Day 2: Sigiriya & Dambulla

Start early from Negombo and visit Sigiriya fort and Dambulla caves and stay in Dambulla hotel.

Leave Negombo very early, drive 3½ hours to Sigiriya, and spend the morning climbing and exploring the rock fortress. Lunch nearby, then continue 45 minutes to Dambulla. Tour the famed cave temple in the afternoon, check into a Dambulla hotel, and unwind—with an optional sunset nature walk if you’re up for it.

Day 3: Kandy & Peradeniya Gardens

Morning, travel from Dambulla to Kandy (about 2 ½ hrs). In Kandy, stroll the scenic Lake round first, then visit the sacred Temple of the Tooth to witness puja rituals and view the revered relic. After lunch, head a few kilometers west to Peradeniya to wander the Royal Botanic Gardens—orchids, giant java fig tree, and palm avenues are highlights. Return to Kandy city for dinner and overnight at your Kandy hotel.

Sandagiri Maha Seya is a newly built large white stupa near Kandy, close to the Hanthana Range and just 6 km from Kandy city center. Situated at about 930 m above sea level, it offers beautiful panoramic views of the mountains and the Mahaweli River valley.

Day 4: Nuwara Eliya & Tea Country

Depart Kandy at dawn and drive south toward Nuwara Eliya. Then continue to the Ramayana-linked sites: Seetha Amman Temple and the nearby Ashok Vatika garden, followed by Diurumpola, the spot associated with Sita’s Agni Pariksha. Resume the scenic ascent through tea country, stopping at an estate and factory to watch Ceylon tea being processed and to sip a fresh cup amid the plantations. Reach the cool highland town of Nuwara Eliya by late afternoon; stroll the lakeside paths of Gregory Lake before settling into your hotel for the night.

Day 5: Ella & Southern Coast

Depart from Nuwara Eliya in the morning and head south toward the southern coast. On the way, stop at Ravana Falls for a short break and photo opportunity. Continue to Ella to visit the famous Nine Arches Bridge, one of Sri Lanka’s most iconic railway landmarks.

The history of the Ravana cave is that King Ravana used this cave to hide the Princess Sita.

After sightseeing, drive onward through the scenic hill country and descend to the southern beaches. Arrive in Weligama or Mirissa by evening and check into your beachside hotel to relax.

Overnight: Hotel in Weligama or Mirissa.

Day 6: Weligama & Mirissa Beach Life

Spend the day enjoying the southern coast. Start with a relaxing morning at Weligama Beach—great for swimming, sunbathing, or beginner surfing. Later, visit Mirissa Beach, just a short drive away, known for its vibrant beach scene, turquoise waters, and stunning views. You can unwind, explore beach cafés, or simply watch the sunset over the Indian Ocean.

The legend of Rumassala is closely linked with the epic tale of Rama and Sita; a story intertwined with much of the history of Sri Lanka.

Day 7: Departure

Check out of your Weligama/Mirissa hotel and drive about 2 ½–3 hours north via the Southern Expressway to Bandaranaike International Airport (CMB).
